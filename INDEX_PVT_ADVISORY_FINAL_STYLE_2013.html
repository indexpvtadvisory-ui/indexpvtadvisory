Investor Charter

Investor Rights & Responsibilities

General investor-awareness guidance presented for clarity. Always review the applicable terms and official regulatory information before acting.

Investor Rights

  • Right to privacy and confidentiality of information shared with the service provider.
  • Right to transparent practices and fair treatment at every stage.
  • Right to adequate information about services, charges and relevant disclosures.
  • Right to fair and truthful communication, free from misleading claims.
  • Right to know expected service timelines and communication procedures.
  • Right to raise concerns and seek timely grievance redressal.
  • Right to exit a service according to the agreed terms.
  • Right to ask questions and seek clarity before acting on research.

Investor Responsibilities

  • Read disclosures, disclaimers and risk information carefully before acting.
  • Undertake independent due diligence; the final investment decision remains yours.
  • Pay service fees only through appropriate banking channels and retain receipts.
  • Ask questions and seek clarification before acting on any recommendation.
  • Keep your trading and Demat login credentials private and secure.
  • Do not rely on unsolicited messages, rumours or claims of guaranteed returns.
  • Report service deficiencies through the appropriate written channel.
  • Understand that market returns are uncertain and cannot be guaranteed.
Investor Awareness

Do's & Don'ts

A simple checklist to help investors approach research and market participation responsibly.

Do's

  • Read the complete research communication before acting.
  • Verify service details and applicable disclosures independently.
  • Pay fees through proper banking channels and keep receipts.
  • Ask questions and clear doubts before taking a decision.
  • Consider your own financial situation and risk tolerance.
  • Report any misleading or guaranteed-return claim through the appropriate channel.

Don'ts

  • Do not hand over funds or account control to another person for investment on your behalf.
  • Don't fall for rumours, unsolicited tips or pressure to act immediately.
  • Do not be influenced only by limited-period discounts or incentives.
  • Never share trading or Demat login credentials.
  • Do not assume past performance guarantees future results.
  • Do not believe anyone promising assured or guaranteed market returns.
